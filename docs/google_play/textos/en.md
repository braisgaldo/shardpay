**Descripcion larga** (1517/4000)

```
Split receipts, not friendships.

ShardPay keeps track of what everyone pays in a group and works out who owes what to whom. No spreadsheets, no arguments, nothing to remember.

READS RECEIPTS WITH THE CAMERA
Photograph the receipt and ShardPay recognises the lines, the amounts and the total. It also checks that the sum matches the printed total, and tells you when it does not. Recognition happens entirely on your phone: photos are never sent to any external service.

SPLIT HOWEVER YOU LIKE
Equally with one tap, or line by line with the exact percentage for each person. Three people pay for the wine, two pay for dessert.

CLEAR BALANCES
ShardPay tells you who owes what, and suggests the smallest number of payments that would settle everything. You can also see your global balance across all your groups.

INVITE IN A SECOND
Share a link or a QR code. You can even add people who do not have an account yet.

YOURS AND NOBODY ELSE'S
No ads. No analytics. No telemetry. Nothing sold to anyone. You can export everything to a file whenever you want, and delete your account from within the app.

14 LANGUAGES AND 13 THEMES
Spanish, English, Galician, Catalan, Basque, French, Italian, Portuguese, German, Greek, Russian, Arabic, Chinese and Japanese. Thirteen colour palettes, each with a light and a dark version, plus an option to follow the system.

ACTUALLY FREE
There is no paid version. There are no reserved features. There is an option to buy the developer a coffee, and it unlocks absolutely nothing.
```
